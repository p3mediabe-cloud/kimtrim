## Stil — kısa

```
Late-night downtempo, 72 BPM, F# minor. Upright bass, brushed rim clicks, Rhodes, tape delay, water and distant ferry ambience. Wordless female humming, few whispered lines. Spacious, nocturnal.
```

## Stil — uzun

```
Late-night downtempo at 72 BPM in F# minor, spacious and unhurried. Upright bass walking slowly, brushed rim clicks instead of a full kit, Rhodes chords drifting through tape delay, a single muted trumpet phrase far back in the mix. Field ambience throughout: water against stone, a distant ferry horn, faint wind on a terrace. Female vocal used as texture more than melody — wordless humming, a handful of near-whispered Turkish lines, long silences between them. Warm analog tape saturation, soft vinyl-like noise floor, very wide reverb, almost no compression. Nothing builds to a climax; it breathes and recedes. Nocturnal, calm, the last hours of a coffee house by the water.
```
